# AI Creative & Workflow Utility Tool (v1.1)

A lightweight, prompt-based utility tool designed to standardize creative production, structure visual layouts, and automate repetitive content tasks across AI platforms.

## Overview
Instead of writing complex instructions from scratch every time, this tool provides modular, reusable frameworks that turn brief inputs into structured, ready-to-use creative and technical outputs.

---

## Core Tool Modules

### 1. Hook & Opening Concept Generator
**Function:** Generates 5 distinct, high-retention opening concepts (under 3 seconds) from any rough topic or idea.
**Input:** Raw topic or target message.
**Output:** Structured list containing the opening line, visual framing cue, and pacing note.

### 2. Visual Scene & Layout Structurer
**Function:** Breaks down any short-form concept into sequential visual blocks for design and animation workflows.
**Input:** Short text or concept outline.
**Output:** Scene-by-scene breakdown specifying:
- On-screen typography hierarchy and placement
- Transition and motion pacing
- Visual focal point and color contrast notes

### 3. Output Quality & Conciseness Refiner (New in v1.1)
**Function:** Takes rough drafts or long instructions and compresses them into clear, actionable, and token-efficient formats without losing key details.
